📋 1. General provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how IndexFast ("we", "us", "our service") collects, uses and protects information you provide when using our service at indexfast.pp.ua.

By using IndexFast, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our service.

In short: we collect the minimum necessary data, do not sell it to third parties, and use it solely to provide IndexFast services.

📦 2. Data we collect

When you register and use the service, we collect:

  • Account data: first name, last name, email address, hashed password.
  • Google Service Account: your JSON service account key for the Google Indexing API. Stored encrypted.
  • Site data: domain name, sitemap URL, indexing status.
  • Indexing logs: page URLs, processing status, HTTP response codes from Google.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, login date and time — for security and diagnostics.
  • Marketing consent: whether you opted in or out of marketing emails at registration (stored to confirm the lawfulness of sending).

⚙️ 3. How we use data

Collected data is used for the following purposes:

3.1. Providing the service

  • Authentication and account management.
  • Submitting URLs for indexing via the Google Indexing API.
  • Displaying indexing statistics and logs.
  • Managing your subscription and plan.

3.2. Transactional emails

We send emails without additional consent, as they are part of delivering the service:

  • Registration and email address confirmation.
  • Password recovery.
  • Notifications about critical service errors (invalid Service Account, etc.).
  • Notifications about plan changes or service updates.

3.3. Marketing emails — consent only

If you ticked the checkbox at registration or gave consent later, we may send:

  • IndexFast product news and updates.
  • Useful SEO and indexing materials.
  • Special offers and plan discounts.
  • Tips for using the service effectively.

Every marketing email contains a one-click unsubscribe link processed immediately. Unsubscribing from marketing does not affect transactional messages.

Your choice: We store the fact of consent given or withdrawn with date and time. You can change your preference at any time in account settings or by writing to indexfastapp@gmail.com.

3.4. Analytics and service improvement

  • Aggregated (anonymised) usage statistics to understand which features need improvement.
  • Diagnosing technical issues.
  • Compliance with applicable law.

We never sell, transfer or exchange your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

🛡️ 4. Storage & security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • Data encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Google API keys stored in an encrypted vault with restricted access.
  • Regular backups and security monitoring.
  • Only authorised personnel have access to personal data.

Data is stored on servers within the EU and/or Ukraine for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion.

🍪 5. Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: authentication session, security settings. The service cannot function without them.
  • Analytical cookies: Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the site. Data is anonymised.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect certain service features.

🔗 6. Third parties

IndexFast interacts with the following third parties:

  • Google LLC — for the Google Indexing API and Google Search Console. Data is transferred in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Payment services (LiqPay / Stripe) — for payment processing. We do not receive or store payment details.
  • Hosting provider — for service hosting. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place.

We do not share your data with other third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

⚖️ 7. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

  1. Access your personal data that we hold.
  2. Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  3. Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  4. Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  5. Receive your data in a machine-readable format (portability).
  6. Withdraw consent to processing at any time (including marketing consent).
  7. Unsubscribe from marketing emails — via the link in each email or in account settings.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at indexfastapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

👶 8. Children

IndexFast is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us immediately for deletion.

🔄 9. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes by email or via an in-app notification at least 14 days before the changes take effect.

The "Last updated" date at the top of the page always reflects the current version of the document.
