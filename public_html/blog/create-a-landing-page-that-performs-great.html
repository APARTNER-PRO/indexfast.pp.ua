How to Create a Landing Page That Performs Great in 2026

More than 60% of your website traffic may end up on a landing page. How to make it not just beautiful, but a working tool that generates leads 24/7?

What is a landing page and why it is critical for business

A landing page is a single-page site created for one goal: to get a client's contact, sell a product, or invite to action.

According to HubSpot, businesses with an optimized landing page get 30% more conversions than those sending traffic to the homepage.

The Ideal Structure of a High-Converting Landing Page

The right structure is 50% of success. Here is a working template:

  1. Hero section (1st screen): H1 heading + subheading + CTA button. Readability in 3 seconds.
  2. Pain/Problem: describe the client's pain briefly and emotionally.
  3. Solution: how you solve this problem, without unnecessary details.
  4. Benefits (3-5 points): not features, but benefits.
  5. Cases/portfolio: screenshots, before/after, numbers.
  6. Pricing/packages: 2-3 options, the middle one is the most profitable.
  7. FAQ: 5-7 frequently asked questions.
  8. Capture form + CTA: name, phone, email. No more than 4 fields.
💡 SEO Secret

Repeat the keyword in H1, first paragraph, H2/H3 subheadings, and alt tags of images.

How to Make a CTA That Gets Clicks

The button is the finale of the user journey. A bad CTA reduces conversion 2-3 times.

  • Use verbs: "Get a consultation", "Download cases", "Start for free".
  • Specificity is better than abstraction: "Order a landing page for $499".
  • Contrasting color: the button must stand out from the background.
  • No more than one CTA per screen.

Landing Page Speed = Conversion

Google researched: increasing load time from 1s to 3s reduces conversion by 32%.

What speeds up a landing page:

  • Reduce images: WebP, lazy loading.
  • Remove unnecessary scripts: metrics, chats, pop-ups.
  • CDN Caching.
  • Asynchronous loading.
  • Optimize Core Web Vitals.

SEO for Landing Pages so Google Loves Them

Landing pages are often created for ads, but organic traffic is forgotten. Add:

  • Title and Description with the keyword.
  • H1 with the main user intent.
  • Alt tags for all images.
  • Schema.org Article or Service.
  • Canonical URL and Open Graph.
  • Internal links.

A/B Testing: Improve Based on Data

Even experienced marketers cannot predict what will work. Test:

  • Headings and subheadings
  • Color and size of the CTA button
  • Order of blocks on the page
  • Heading formulas
  • Presence/absence of reviews

Tools: VWO, Optimizely. Test one variable at a time.

Mobile Version: 60% of Your Client's Traffic

Most users visit landing pages from their phones. Check:

  • Font not less than 14px
  • CTA button not less than 48x48px
  • The form doesn't go beyond the screen
  • No horizontal scrolling
  • Loading from 3G - no more than 5 seconds

5 Mistakes That Destroy Conversion

  • Too much text. A landing page is not an article.
  • No social proof. Add reviews, client logos.
  • How many CTAs? Only one!
  • Not updating content.
  • Forgot about indexing. The landing page will not be found if Google doesn't index it.
