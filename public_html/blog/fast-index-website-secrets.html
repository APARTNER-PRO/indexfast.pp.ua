Why indexing is the first step to profit

Every SEO specialist knows: quality content and proper meta tags are worthless if the page is not in the search database. Website indexing is the first and most important step to getting traffic. But what to do when Google bots ignore your updates?

This is a problem faced by thousands of website owners. You optimize content, add meta tags, improve structure — and Google doesn't rush to index new pages. Meanwhile, competitors have already taken your positions in search results.

What is Index Follow and why is it important?

At the basic level, the index and follow meta tags tell search engines that a page can be added to the database and that links on it can be followed. This is a basic signal for Googlebot: "this page is worth indexing".

However, the presence of these tags only gives permission, but does not force the bot to come immediately. Index follow is not a command, but a recommendation. Google decides for itself when and whether to add a page to the index, based on its crawl budget and priorities.

Key factors for fast indexing (Site Indexation)

To achieve the fastest possible site indexation, several important factors need to be considered:

Content quality. Unique and useful text is indexed better. Googlebot allocates more budget to pages that provide real value to users.

Internal linking. Helps bots find new pages more easily. If a new article is linked from popular sections, Google will find it faster.

Loading speed. Fast sites are crawled better (Crawl Budget). The faster a page loads, the more pages Googlebot can scan in a single visit.

Use of specialized tools. The last point often becomes decisive. If you are looking for a way to make index your site faster, pay attention to professional tools, so-called indexers.
