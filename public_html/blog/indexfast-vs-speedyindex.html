IndexFast vs SpeedyIndex: Which is the Best Indexing Tool in 2026?

🗓 Published: June 28, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

If you're looking for an automated solution to index your new web pages or backlinks quickly, you've likely come across both IndexFast and SpeedyIndex. Both tools aim to solve the same problem: bypassing the slow wait times of traditional Googlebot crawling.

However, their underlying technologies, pricing models, and target audiences differ significantly. In this comprehensive comparison, we break down the key differences to help you choose the best tool for your SEO strategy.

Core Methodology: How Do They Work?

The biggest difference between the two platforms is how they attempt to force Google to index your links.

IndexFast: The Official API Route

IndexFast is built strictly around the official Google Indexing API and the IndexNow protocol (for Bing/Yandex). This means you connect your Google Search Console account, and IndexFast directly pings Google's servers using Google's own approved methods. It's safe, instantly registers, and is highly recommended for tier-1 sites and main domains.

SpeedyIndex: The Network/Bot Route

SpeedyIndex traditionally relies on proprietary networks, bot emulation, and mobile-friendly test pings to force Googlebot to discover URLs. It doesn't require Google Search Console access. This makes it popular for gray-hat SEOs indexing thousands of mass-generated backlinks on sites they don't own, but it carries higher risks and varying success rates depending on Google's algorithm updates.

Pricing and Quotas

Pricing structures differ vastly between the two services.

  • IndexFast offers a generous free tier that manages Google's native 200 URL/day quota. The PRO plan ($9.99/mo) unlocks automation, sitemap monitoring, and premium support. It's a flat-rate SaaS model.
  • SpeedyIndex uses a pay-as-you-go credit system. You pay for packages of links (e.g., 10,000 links for a set price). While cheap in bulk, it becomes expensive if you are constantly updating a massive e-commerce site every day.

The Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose IndexFast if:

  • You want to index your own websites (where you have Google Search Console access).
  • You are running a white-hat operation, e-commerce store, blog, or SaaS.
  • You want a "set it and forget it" solution that automatically monitors your sitemap.
  • You prefer using 100% official, safe Google methods.

Choose SpeedyIndex if:

  • You need to index thousands of low-quality backlinks on websites you don't own.
  • You are doing aggressive churn-and-burn gray-hat SEO.
  • You do not have Search Console access to the URLs you are submitting.
