IndexFast vs SpeedyIndex: Which is the Best Indexing Tool in 2026?

SpeedyIndex vs IndexFast: Who is the Leader in 2026?

The indexing problem remains a headache for many SEO specialists. Even the best content or the most expensive backlink has no value until Googlebot visits the page and adds it to the index.

There are two popular approaches to forced indexing on the market: using bot networks (as SpeedyIndex does) and using the official Google Indexing API (the IndexFast approach). In this review, we will compare these two services in detail.

SpeedyIndex Review: How Does It Work?

SpeedyIndex is a Telegram bot and web panel that uses the so-called "network method" of indexing. Their technology involves massively running your links through hundreds of web resources, Google accounts, and bot networks to force Google crawlers to pay attention to your URLs.

  • Pros: Works without the need to connect Search Console. Can "push through" low-quality links.
  • Cons: A gray-hat SEO method that carries risks for main domains. The indexing speed often takes from 48 hours to a week.

IndexFast Review: Official API Method

IndexFast works fundamentally differently. Instead of spam methods, the service connects to your Google Search Console and sends direct POST requests via the official Google Indexing API. This is a legal (white-hat) way to tell Google: "Here is a new page, come and crawl it now".

  • Pros: 100% safe for the site. The highest possible speed (usually within 24 hours).
  • Cons: Requires access to Search Console (which takes 5 minutes during the initial setup).

What to Choose?

If you are promoting a high-quality business project, a client site, or your own online store, using SpeedyIndex can be risky. Google's algorithms are becoming stricter regarding artificial attention manipulation.

IndexFast is the choice for those who value safety and results. Thanks to the official API, you communicate directly with Google's servers, which guarantees the fastest and most reliable indexing of your pages without any penalties.
