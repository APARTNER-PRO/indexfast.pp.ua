🚀 2. Description of service

IndexFast is a tool for automatically submitting your website's URLs for indexing to Google via the official Google Indexing API.

  • Reading URLs from a sitemap.xml file (including sitemap index).
  • Bulk submission of URLs via the Google Indexing API within Google's quotas.
  • Maintaining logs and indexing status reports.
  • Automated scheduled runs (on appropriate plans).
Important: IndexFast acts as an intermediary between your site and the Google API. We do not guarantee specific search rankings — that is solely determined by Google.
