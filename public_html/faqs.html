Everything you need to know about Google Search Console indexing and IndexFast.

8 languages 200 URLs/day free quota 24–48h average indexing time Updated June 2026

IndexFast is a web service that automates page indexing in Google through the official Google Indexing API. Instead of manually submitting URLs one by one in Google Search Console, you connect your GSC account once, add a sitemap, and IndexFast automatically submits all your pages for indexing.

It works by creating a Service Account in Google Cloud and granting it access to your GSC property. IndexFast then uses the official indexing.googleapis.com endpoint to notify Google about new or updated pages.

Once submitted through the Google Indexing API, Google typically crawls and indexes pages within 24–48 hours — compared to the usual 2–8 weeks with regular crawling.

For maximum speed, combine IndexFast with:

  • A fresh sitemap.xml submitted in GSC
  • Pages with proper meta tags and structured data
  • Regular content updates to keep the site active in Google's eyes

Setup takes about 10 minutes:

  • Step 1: Add your site in Google Search Console and verify ownership
  • Step 2: Register at indexfast.pro
  • Step 3: Create a project in Google Cloud Console
  • Step 4: Enable the Indexing API in your project
  • Step 5: Create a Service Account and download the JSON key
  • Step 6: Add the Service Account email as an Owner in GSC → Settings → Users & permissions
  • Step 7: Upload the JSON key in IndexFast dashboard

Yes. The Google Indexing API supports two actions:

  • URL_UPDATED — tells Google the page content has changed
  • URL_DELETED — tells Google to remove the page from the index

When IndexFast detects a new or modified URL in your sitemap, it sends an URL_UPDATED notification. This signals Google to re-crawl the page much faster than waiting for discovery crawl.
