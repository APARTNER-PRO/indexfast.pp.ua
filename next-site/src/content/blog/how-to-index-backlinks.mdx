In 2026, Google is heavily rationing its crawl budget. It simply ignores millions of new URLs every day if it deems them low priority. Here is how you can force Google to discover and index your backlinks.


      
## Method 1: The "Tier 2" Link Method (Safe &amp; Organic)

      
Google bots follow links. If you build a backlink on <em>Website A</em>, but no one ever links to that specific page on <em>Website A</em>, Googlebot might never find it.

      
The solution is to build a **Tier 2 link**. You create a link from a site that Google already crawls frequently (like Twitter, Reddit, or your own active blog) and point it directly to the URL containing your backlink. Once Google crawls the Tier 2 link, it will discover your Tier 1 backlink.


      <div>
        **💡 Best Practice**
        
Use active social media profiles to post a link to the guest post that contains your backlink. Google frequently scans social media sites, ensuring rapid discovery of the new URL.

      </div>

      
## Method 2: Google Indexing API (If you own the site)

      
If you are building an internal backlink network or creating PBNs (Private Blog Networks) where you have Google Search Console access to the linking domains, **you should always use the Google Indexing API**.

      
By connecting your properties to an automated tool like IndexFast, you can push the API to index your new posts instantly. Because it is an official Google API, the success rate is near 100% and it happens within 24 hours.


      
## Method 3: Third-Party Indexing Tools (For Guest Posts)

      
What if you bought a guest post on a high-DR site, but you obviously don't have their Google Search Console password? You can't use the Google Indexing API directly.

      
In this scenario, SEOs often turn to bot-emulation indexing tools. Tools like SpeedyIndex or Omega Indexer use proprietary networks of blogs to artificially ping Googlebot. While these methods carry some risk if overused, they are currently the most reliable way to force the indexing of URLs you do not own.


      <div>
        **⚠️ Don't use ping tools for Money Sites**
        
Never use third-party "bot network" indexing tools on your primary business domain. Only use them for off-page backlinks. For your main site, stick exclusively to the Google Indexing API.

      </div>
